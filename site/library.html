Which one do I use?

Skills that look alike

One meeting, or many sprints?

The event skills prepare one meeting today. scrum-workflow runs a team's sprints over time and remembers them between sessions.

Which retro skill?

scrum-retrospective plans and facilitates the meeting. sprint-retrospective builds a data pack from git history to bring to it.

Blocker or risk?

impediment-log for something blocking the sprint now. pre-mortem for a launch that could fail. project-manager for a project's risk register.
